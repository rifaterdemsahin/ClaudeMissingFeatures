🧰 What to do to prepare (and what each saves)

#DoSaves
1Install and check everything first (check_env.sh); download the Whisper model once5 to 10 min on the first video
2Create the Azure container and Key Vault secrets once (setup_azure_exports.sh)one blocked step later
3Start Whisper as soon as the sync is done; it runs while the base video rendersabout 4 min
4Keep the machine idle while Remotion renders (no OCR, no builds, no tests)10 to 20 min per render: 13 vs 5 to 9 frames/s
5Make the base video with the index at the front (faststart)about 35% of render time
6Collect all review notes, then render once per round28 min per avoided render
7Use quick previews: a still for one moment, or render --scale=0.5 / --frames=a-b for a sectionmost of a full render
8Pick the LinkedIn and X highlight ranges while the YouTube render runs, then queue all platforms in one commandno idle gaps, one hand-off
9Run the privacy check on the YouTube file while choosing platform cuts4 min of waiting
10Keep caches: OCR, transcript and the registry are reused, never redone4 to 5 min per round
11Fill project.json (title, community, sensitive words) and okr.json before editingre-renders caused by a missed word or name
12Keep the recording clean: no notifications, no other windows, quiet roomblur work, a hoover cut, apology cards
